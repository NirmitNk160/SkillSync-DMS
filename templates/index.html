ABOUT SKILLSYNC

Bridging Skills with Opportunities

SkillSync is a skill-based career matching platform developed as a case study for Discrete Mathematical Structures. It helps students discover internships and job opportunities based on their technical skills. Using Set Theory, SkillSync identifies matching skills, highlights missing requirements, and calculates a match percentage.

Set Intersection

Identifies the skills shared by the student and the job requirements.

A ∩ B

Set Difference

Identifies the skills required by the job but missing from the student.

B − A

Cardinality

Counts matching skills and helps calculate the overall match percentage.

|A ∩ B|

Our Goal

To simplify career discovery for students and demonstrate how mathematical concepts can solve real-world problems.
